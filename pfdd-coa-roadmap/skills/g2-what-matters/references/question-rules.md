# Question rules (G2 §III.B, §IV.B, App. 3 Table 5; G3 App. B)

`scripts/lint_questions.py` screens for the machine-detectable ones; judgment covers the rest.

| Rule | Why | G2's own example | Better |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Don't lead** — don't suggest an answer or a preferred answer | Biased/false answers; missed unexpected insight | "Wouldn't you consider it most important to improve your walking distance, for example, how far you walk around the track when you exercise?" | "Tell me how peripheral artery disease impacts you. What would you like to see improve with treatment?" |
| **Don't cast judgment** on beliefs or choices | Participant becomes defensive | "Could you tell me why you are not treating your condition with medication?" | "What did you consider when deciding whether to treat your condition with medication?" |
| **One concept per question** (no double-/multi-barreled) | Can't tell which concept was answered | "How embarrassed and self-conscious have you been because of your condition?" | Two questions: embarrassed; self-conscious |
| **No double negatives** | Misread; hard by phone/IVR; hard to translate | "I do not have symptoms that are not of concern." | "I have symptoms that are of concern." |
| **No incomplete questions** | Ambiguous | "Age? Reason last saw doctor?" | Full sentences |
| **Plain, patient language** | Understanding across literacy levels | "dyspnea" | "shortness of breath" |
| **Concrete, own-experience framing** | Abstract questions yield abstract answers | — | Diary ("walk me through a typical day"), critical incident ("tell me about the worst time"), free listing, ranking |
| **Observer, not proxy**, for caregivers | Proxy reports of inner states are unreliable | "How severe was your child's pain…?" (proxy) | "How often did you see your child holding their stomach?" (observed) |
| **Open before closed; general before specific** | Avoid priming and order bias | — | Spontaneous mention first, then probes |

Survey items additionally (G2 §IV.B): appropriate for literacy/health literacy/numeracy; natural
language; translatable; applicable (allow N/A where needed); cognitively tested; simple format;
checked for social-desirability bias; scripted if interviewer-administered; avoid VAS
(can't be read aloud, more missing data — G2 Table 3, G3 §IV.E.1); watch priming from question
order; screening questions only where they prevent irrelevant items.

## Response options (G2 Table 3; G3 §IV.E.1)
Non-overlapping; adjacent categories reflect true gradations; wording clear and concrete. Prefer
verbal rating scales (none/mild/moderate/severe) or 0–10 numeric rating scales over VAS.
